===============================================================
   File-to-QR Video Transmission Protocol Demo
   Date: 2026-10-14
===============================================================

This is a demonstration of optical air-gapped data transfer using dynamic QR codes.
The system breaks arbitrary binary or text files into chunks, encapsulates them
with self-describing metadata headers and SHA-256 checksums, and renders them into
an H.264 MP4 video stream.

Key features verified in this test:
1. Automatic Gzip compression for payload optimization.
2. Base64 encoding for universal scanner compatibility.
3. Top and bottom visual progress banners on each frame.
4. Inter-frame hold resilience against video codec compression.
5. Early termination on decoder once 100% of chunks are collected.
6. Bit-for-bit SHA-256 cryptographic verification upon reconstruction.
